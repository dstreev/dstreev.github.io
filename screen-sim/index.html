Course and retrieval lists are 100 px tall because that is the size set in createCourseTab(). The top-right state word is gone. The buoy-status box hides with Deploy and Retrieve while Virtual Course is active. Calibrate Touch has no click handler in the firmware. The LoRa tab stays at placeholder RSSI; the status bar is the only place that shows LoRa:OK. Drive is the water HUD: horizon, dashed gate lines, heading path, buoys, and the off-course meter. Fifteen blocks each side: eight at 6 in, four at 1 ft, three at 3 ft. Inside 1 ft the lit blocks are green, inside 2 ft yellow, inside 4 ft orange, and farther out red. Heading off course is positive to the right. Opening, Meets ahead, and Overshoot park the boat 2.1 m left. Virtual, not locked, is heading-hold with no buoys. Width runs from 7.5 m mini to 11.5 m full in half-meter steps and scales the skier buoys only. Extension runs from 1.0x to 1.5x in tenths and stretches buoy stations along the course from the anchor. It does not change width. The 55s, gates, and boat guides keep their official offsets from centerline. Ant fore, Ant stbd, and Bow are the hull-LED boat measurements. They do not move the picture. The pass plot stays on official spacing. A buoy joins when it is turned on and registers. On a plotted course the sky names the registered buoys that are not on station. Center Line is the default retrieval point. Retrieve sends each buoy to the closest point on that course's centerline. Rename and delete do not apply to it. An active waypoint is still the recall destination when one is set. Setup Timing sets each live interval from one tenth to ten times the shipped rate. The middle is today's rate. GPS and LoRa stay on every pass. The 14:32 pass plot stays at 5 samples a second. Users is the first Setup section. Each person has a preferred rope and a choice to shorten that rope after a pass that reaches the exit. Home shows Driver beside Deploy and Skier beside Retrieve, with a gap between the rows. Drive sits at the bottom. Retrieve uses the same green face as Deploy. Driver and Skier stay on screen when Virtual Course hides Deploy and Retrieve. Picking a skier sets the rope line and the Home shorten switch from that person's preference. The switch overrides shortening for this set and does not rewrite the user. Pass finished, under the bezel, stands in for the boat reaching the exit gates and shortens the line when the switch is on. Config, Set reset, is how long a break with no pass waits before the rope and the switch return to that skier's preference. The left end is Off. The default is 15 minutes. Each new pass keeps the names that were selected. Passes can filter by driver, skier, or both. Deleting a user asks first: saved passes keep the stored name, and a later feature that points at that user will no longer find them. The sim remembers the list in this browser. Course and buoy status share the top row, course on the left. Rope Length is a left-to-right line of radio marks under Skier. MPH shows feet off (15 through 41, with 39.5). Metric shows meters. The live choice stays in this browser and is written on each pass. Drive shows speed at the top center in grey, in mph or kph. Passes lists recorded hull passes, newest date and newest time first, then plots the boat left to right. The center line is where the boat should be. The outside lines are the boat guides at 1.15 m, with a 0.5 m scale. Small circles mark the 55s (green), gates and skier balls (red), and boat guides (yellow). Skier balls sit in the margin so the 1.15 m scale stays readable. The 14:32 pass is 34 mph at 5 samples a second. Driver and skier for that pass are David and Beth.
